FAQ

Questions students ask us first

Can I study abroad without IELTS?

Yes — in many destinations around the world, several universities offer routes with or without IELTS, and some let you submit the English certificate later. In some destinations, for example, universities let you submit the certificate well after the application deadline, so you can apply now and prepare for IELTS in parallel.

That said, a good IELTS score (6.0–6.5) strengthens both your admission and your visa file. In your free counselling session we'll tell you honestly whether your target universities need it.

Are there really fully funded scholarships?

Yes — some destinations around the world have government scholarships, and some are awarded mainly on family income, not on top marks. For eligible students they can cover the full tuition fee, accommodation, meals and a yearly cash grant — which is why students call them "fully funded".

You apply for it after admission and enrolment, with family income documents prepared from Pakistan. We guide that whole file. One thing to plan for: the destination's student visa may still ask you to show proof of funds, as required by its own rules, before you travel — the scholarship money reaches you after arrival.

How much does your counselling cost?

Your first counselling session is completely free — bring your documents and questions, and leave with a clear plan and honest chances. If you then choose to process your applications with us, every fee (ours, the university's and the visa's) is explained in writing before you commit to anything.

When should I apply to study abroad?

Application deadlines vary by destination and intake, so the right time to apply depends on where you want to study — in some destinations applications close many months before the intake begins. As a rule, start early: give yourself time to prepare documents, take any English test you need, and meet every deadline without rushing.

Some destinations are strict about English scores — for example, an overall IELTS of 6.5 with no band below 5.5. If you haven't taken it yet, book the computer-based test now; results come in 1–5 days. In your free counselling session we'll map out the exact deadlines for your chosen destination.

What documents do I need to get started?

Just four things for your first session: your passport, your latest transcript, your degree or certificate, and a CV in English (Europass format is widely recognised across Europe — we can help you build one). Everything else — motivation letters, attestations, income documents for scholarships — we prepare with you step by step.
